MEDICAL STUDENT • SOFTWARE DEVELOPER

Kay/Koichi is a third-year medical student at the University of Manchester, pursuing Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBChB) degree. He graduated from the School of Medicine, University of St Andrews in July 2025 with Bachelor of Science Honours (Medicine). Along with his major in medicine he is a lead software developer at Larry Education Inc., and developed several systems and apps. He is a member of multiple research projects led by a professor at Institute of Science Tokyo, and has published conference and journal papers, with 2 awards at the world conferences.
